## What's new in 0.7.0

### Programme guide
- Several EPG sources per channel are merged instead of replacing each other: broadcast EIT, provider EPG (MediaHighway / Freesat), DVB-I content guide and XMLTV (#46)
- Automatic mode: now and next come from the broadcast, further ahead the most complete source wins, gaps are filled from the other sources, no programme appears twice, and missing details are borrowed from the same programme in another source (#46)
- Preferences ▸ EPG ▸ Advanced: a custom order of the sources, for all channels or per channel (#46)

### On-screen info
- A slim quality bar at the top of the channel info: empty without signal, then red, orange, yellow or green. DVB channels use lock, SNR, signal level and uncorrected blocks of the tuner; internet channels show the state of the stream (buffer fill, recent stalls) (#47)
