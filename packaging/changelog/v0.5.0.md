## What's new in 0.5.0

### Radio visualisations
- Three new painted scenes, drawn entirely on the graphics card: Supernova (modern), Occasus solis (modern) and Aurora borealis (modern) (#39)
- Every movement in them comes from the music: they drift with quiet music, surge forward on the beat and stand still in silence (#39)
- Supernova: veils of glowing gas are born in the white core and drift outwards; their strength and colours come from the music at their birth. When the music stops the veils thin out, a last flash fills the screen and a single star is left twinkling (#39)
- Occasus solis: a sunset with rays turning around the sun and clouds grown from the music drifting from left to right; clouds cast shafts of light. When the music stops the clouds dissolve, the rays fade and the sun sets (#39)
- Aurora borealis: a band of northern lights over snowy mountains that are grown from the music and scroll from right to left. When the music stops the aurora fades, the stars dim and the mountains sink away (#39)
