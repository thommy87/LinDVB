## What's new in 0.8.0

### Hardware video decoding
- Preferences ▸ Playback ▸ Video decoding: Automatic uses the graphics card when it can (VA-API for Intel and AMD, then NVDEC for NVIDIA, then V4L2 on ARM boards) and decodes in software otherwise; VA-API, NVDEC, Vulkan Video, V4L2 or software can also be chosen by hand. The page shows the graphics card decoders found and the decoder playing now (#49)
- When a graphics card decoder fails (a driver bug), LinDVB switches it off for the session and plays on with the next decoder, finally in software (#49)
- With VA-API, deinterlacing runs on the graphics card as well (#49)
- The AppImage now includes the hardware decoder plugins and uses the system's VA-API, NVIDIA and Vulkan drivers (#49)
