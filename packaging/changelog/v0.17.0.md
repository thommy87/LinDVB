## What's new in 0.17.0

### Internet TV
- Timeshift for internet channels: M3U, Xtream, DVB-I and multicast channels now go through the same timeshift buffer as a tuner, so pause, jump back, "Keep Timeshift as Recording" and recordings with teletext and EPG data work for them too. HLS and DASH still switch quality with the connection; the buffer keeps what arrived. Preferences > Playback > Timeshift can keep them on the best quality instead (#120)
- Broken connections of internet and Tvheadend streams are opened again after a few seconds, for up to three minutes (#120)
- Catch-up: "Watch from the Start" in the programme guide plays programmes of the last days from the provider's archive, for M3U lists with catchup attributes and Xtream Codes servers with an archive (#120)
- Tvheadend servers as a channel source: channels, channel tags as groups, logos and the programme guide, with streams through LinDVB's own timeshift buffer, recordings, teletext and HbbTV (Preferences > Internet > IPTV > Tvheadend) (#119)

### Channels
- Channel list filter by source: satellite, cable, terrestrial, IPTV and DiSEqC port (#115)
- Channel editor: favourites lists beside the full channel list, your own channel order, drag and drop, and clearer DVB-I source names (#117)
- Export: choose the channels (all, TV, radio, shown, one favourites list) and the format, including LinDVB .json (#114)

### Preferences
- The tuner preferences are split into Hardware Tuners and SAT>IP (#118)

### Languages
- Japanese translation (#116)

### Packaging
- Snap: built by Canonical for amd64 and arm64 and only promoted at a release (#108, #109, #112)
