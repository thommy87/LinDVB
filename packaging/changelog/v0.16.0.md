## What's new in 0.16.0

### Channels
- Add channels from a single frequency: Preferences > Channels > Add Channels opens a dialog to tune one frequency by hand. Choose the tuner (only the ones present) and the reception type, enter the frequency and, as needed, satellite and DiSEqC port (preset from the channel search), polarisation, symbol rate, system, modulation, FEC, bandwidth or PLP; for satellites a transponder of the list can be picked to fill the fields. Lock, signal level and quality are shown while tuning (#106)
- The channels found are compared with the channel list for this transponder only: unchanged (grey), changed (orange, with what changed), new (green) and no longer broadcast (red). Each line has a tick, and Save adds, updates and removes the ticked ones (#106)

### Visualisations
- With "Draw on the graphics card" off, supernova, occasus solis and aurora borealis now follow the music as their OpenGL versions do (#105)

### Changes
- The plain GOOM visualisation is removed; Goom HD stays, and a saved choice of GOOM switches to it (#105)

### Fixes
- Channel search: a search of single transponders no longer adds the frequencies the network announces (#106)
