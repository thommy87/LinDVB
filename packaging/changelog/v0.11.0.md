## What's new in 0.11.0

### DVB-I by country
- The DVB-I settings (Preferences ▸ Internet) have a country choice. Each country brings its own registry, privacy notice and consents, region and preferred languages; the settings of each country are kept and come back when you switch to it again (#62)
- Germany: the DTVP profile as before, with postcode or nationwide list (#62)
- Italy (HD Forum Italia UHD Book 2.2): regione from a list (19 regions and the autonomous provinces of Trento and Bolzano), optional CAP that suggests the regione, privacy information under art. 13 GDPR and optional, withdrawable consents for audience measurement and personalised advertising. Italian channel names come first, and a channel you receive terrestrially uses that before the internet stream (#62)
- New Zealand (Freeview NextGen): Freeview region, optional postcode, consent for anonymous viewing information, broadcast and internet channels merged with the broadcast copy first (#62)
- The registry addresses can be changed in the settings (#62)
- HbbTV applications are told the DVB-I country, and the youth protection steps follow it (Germany 6/12/16, Italy 6/14, New Zealand 13/16). Without the audience measurement consent, applications see Do Not Track (#62)
- The privacy notices are translated (German and Italian) (#62)
