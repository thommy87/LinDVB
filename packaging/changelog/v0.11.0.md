## What's new in 0.11.0

### DVB-I by country
- The DVB-I settings (Preferences ▸ Internet) have a country choice. Each country brings its own registry, region and preferred languages, and shows a privacy notice only where its profile asks for one; the settings of each country are kept and come back when you switch to it again (#62)
- Germany: the DTVP profile as before, with privacy notice and postcode or nationwide list (#62)
- Italy (HD Forum Italia UHD Book 2.2): the Italian registry with TargetCountry=ITA, regione from a list (19 regions and the autonomous provinces of Trento and Bolzano) with an optional CAP that suggests it, channels without a number in the Main Overflow 850–999, Italian channel names first (#62)
- New Zealand (Freeview NextGen): Freeview region, optional postcode, broadcast and internet channels merged with the broadcast copy first (#62)
- Lists with subscription packages: choose the package whose channel numbers are used (#62)
- The automatic daily update of the DVB-I list can be switched off (#62)
- The registry addresses can be changed in the settings (#62)
- HbbTV applications are told the DVB-I country, and the youth protection steps follow it (Germany 6/12/16, Italy PIN from 14 and 18, New Zealand 13/16) (#62)
