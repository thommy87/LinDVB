## What's new in 0.10.0

### Channel list
- The channel list filter (for example Favourites) and the group come back at the next start, with the last channel selected in the list (#60)

### Channel editor
- Every row of the channel list in the editor has a favourite switch; the switch in the details pane is gone (#60)
- Edits to several channels are all kept and applied together with one click on the checkmark (Ctrl+S). Before, selecting another channel dropped the edits of the previous one, so only one channel got saved. Edited channels are marked with ✎ and the window title counts them; closing the editor with edits left asks whether to apply or discard them (#60)
- Just looking at a channel no longer changes values the editor's lists cannot show, such as an unusual modulation (#60)
- Moving a channel up or down keeps it selected, also with a filter (#60)
