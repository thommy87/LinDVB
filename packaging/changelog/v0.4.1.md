## What's new in 0.4.1

### Radio visualisations
- projectM works on Wayland desktops: it no longer fails with "Subclass failed to initialize" and a black screen, and the music no longer stops (#37)
- If a visualisation cannot start, the music keeps playing, Goom HD takes over and the status bar says why (#37)
- projectM is one entry with a submenu: all presets shuffled, or a single category (Dancer, Fractal, …) or subfolder (#37)
- The names of the visualisations are translated (#37)
- The AppImage uses the stable libprojectM 4.1.8 (#37)

### Preferences
- The Tuner and LNB page is now called Tuner and Channels; the Recording page has its own translation (#36)

### Plugins
- The Plugins window shows your plugin folder with an Open Plugin Folder button instead of internal paths (#35)
- Plugins can offer choices for a visualisation in a submenu (`add_visualizer(..., choices=…)`) (#37)
