## What's new in 0.15.0

### IPTV
- M3U lists in m3u_plus / m3u8_plus form are imported reliably: lists that start with a byte order mark (common for Xtream `get.php` downloads), lists without the `#EXTM3U` header and files called `.m3uplus`, `.m3u8plus`, `.m3u_plus` or `.m3u8_plus` no longer end with "no channels" (#99)
- Each imported list has its own name: new lists are named after the server and user (Xtream) or the host instead of "get" or "index", and a "Rename" button in Preferences > Internet > IPTV changes the name, which is also the list's channel group (#99)
- Xtream Codes: choose MPEG-TS or HLS streams; films and series of the list are left out, only the live channels are imported (#99)
- Channel names with commas are no longer cut off; `#EXTGRP` groups and `url|User-Agent=…` addresses are understood (#99)

### Channel search
- Search satellites one by one: tick the positions to search, the others keep their settings (#98)
- While searching, the lock, signal level and quality (C/N) are shown, also for SAT>IP tuners (#98)
- Transponder editor per satellite position: search single transponders, add, change or remove them; the changed list is kept and used instead of the bundled one (#98)

### Start
- Preferences > Channels > At start: the last channel watched, the first channel of the list, a chosen channel, or no channel (black screen) (#97)

### Changes
- projectM is taken out until libprojectM and gst-projectm are fixed; it stayed black, showed fragments or froze on many systems. GOOM stays; a saved choice of projectM falls back to the default visualisation

### Fixes
- Channel search: circular polarisation is written as LEFT / RIGHT in scan files; it was written as VERTICAL, which gave the wrong LNB voltage for L (#98)
- A startup channel that makes the receiver fail no longer locks LinDVB at every start (#97)
