## What's new in 0.3.0

### Languages
- The interface is translatable and follows the desktop language; it can also be chosen in Preferences (#30)
- Translations for all 24 official EU languages, plus Arabic (right to left), Russian and Ukrainian (#30)

### TV systems
- ATSC: programme guide from PSIP, virtual channel numbers (e.g. 7.1) and closed captions (CEA-608/708) (#30)
- ISDB-T, DTMB and US cable channel lists are scanned with the right delivery system (#30)

### Recordings and guide
- Recordings tab as a library: details, previews and continue watching (#30)
- Reminders for programmes in the guide, and export of the guide as XMLTV (#30)

### Player and desktop
- Picture in picture and a channel mosaic for internet channels (#30)
- Media keys and desktop media controls (MPRIS), plus script hooks for your own automations (#30)

### Testing
- Channel search, tuning and EPG are tested with the kernel's virtual tuner vidtv (#30)
