## What's new in 0.12.0

### Watching and recording
- Watch another channel of the same transponder while a recording runs on that tuner, with HbbTV for the channel you watch (#72, #73)
- Preferred audio format: stereo, AC-3 or E-AC-3 (#72)
- Record with options: choose format and tracks for one recording (#72)
- Recordings can keep all streams of the channel, with a cleaned PAT (#74)
- Timers: general settings or options of their own, for the whole series or only the next recording (#74)
- Internet streams (HLS) start on a good quality instead of climbing through every variant, and only stop to buffer when less than 3 seconds are left (#78)

### Channels and tuners
- Channel list sorting by number, name, provider, transponder or picture quality (#74)
- The preferences have separate Tuners, Channels and HbbTV pages. Tuners show the name of the card or stick (e.g. "TerraTec Cinergy S2 PCIe Dual"), can get your own name, and combined tuners can be set to the socket they are connected to. Each satellite tuner has its own LNB (#77)
- Picons: logos named by service reference or SNP name are found in the logo folder and shown in the channel list too (#77)
- IPTV: a table of the imported lists with update and remove, single HLS streams, tvg-logo, Xtream Codes login (#74)
- Own DVB-I service list addresses for any country (#71)

### Interface
- Colour scheme: dark, light or the same as the system (#75)
- New preferences page for the OSD and visualisations, subtitle font, keyboard shortcuts, teletext button, reworked menu and timer window (#71)
- Smoother projectM visualisation (#72)
- Persian translation, right to left (#76)

### Fixes
- Screen saver, Video Off on internet channels, HbbTV button, channel analysis layout and the bit rate of internet radio (#70)

### Tools
- `tools/hls-diagnose.py` measures dropouts of an internet stream (#77, #78)
