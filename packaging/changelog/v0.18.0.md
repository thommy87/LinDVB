## What's new in 0.18.0

### HbbTV
- Teletext links of the HbbTV launcher start the right application, and the application now loads automatically when a channel starts if you want it to: off by default, and 1.5 s after a zap so zapping through channels loads no pages (#129)
- HLS and DASH videos in HbbTV applications play instead of staying black; a video that fails says so in the status bar (#129)

### Programme guide
- Clearer programme details: Watch, Record and Remind Me as buttons, a larger title, channel, time, genre and age as chips, and a description with Show more / Show less (#130)
- Fixed column widths that no longer jump while scrolling and are remembered; Per Channel shows the channel focused in Now / Next (#131)

### Playback
- Anamorphic SD channels (16:9 at 720x576) keep their shape in the software video output used by the AppImage and the snap (#123)
- Space is play / pause only, even when a button has the keyboard focus (#128)

### Channels
- Tvheadend: channel tags become groups instead of the picture quality (HDTV, SDTV), and a channel with several tags is listed under each of them (#124, #111)

### Interface
- Preferences: titles and hints above the fields where rows hold several entries (IPTV servers, station logos) (#131, #132)
- The main menu is shorter: the view and playback items live in the gear menu (#132, #133)

### Languages
- Georgian, traditional Chinese and simplified Chinese translations (#125)
