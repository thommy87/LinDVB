## What's new in 0.4.0

### Radio visualisations
- projectM (MilkDrop) is included in the AppImage, with about 9,800 presets and the MilkDrop textures; it appears in the visualisation picker without installing anything (#32)
- projectM presets are found again when gst-projectm is installed on the system (#32)
- Goom HD: the ball of the amulette and crystal modes is shown completely, with a margin at the top and bottom; tentacles, scope lines and fireworks are drawn smoothly (#32)

### Preferences
- Tuner and LNB has a Channels section with the channel search and the channel editor / favourites (#31)
- New Internet page: DVB-I setup and import of one or more M3U / IPTV channel lists (#31)
- The TV menu is tidier; Shift+Ctrl+S and Shift+Ctrl+E still open the channel search and editor (#31)
