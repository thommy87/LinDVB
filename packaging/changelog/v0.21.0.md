## What's new in 0.21.0

### Languages
- Albanian (Shqip): the whole interface is translated; HbbTV applications and XMLTV guides get the Albanian language code too (#184)

### Snap
- Internet TV and radio play again in the snap on desktops with xdg-desktop-portal (e.g. GNOME): the proxy lookup through the portal was refused, so every HTTP and HTTPS stream failed at once. 0.20.2 in the stable channel is affected (#182)

### AppImage
- The AppImage now brings the GStreamer plugins for the loudness meter, for AAC and subtitles in MP4/MKV recordings and for closed captions (CEA-708) (#183)

### Flatpak
- Closed captions (CEA-608/708) show in the Flatpak too: it now brings the closed caption plugin (#186)

### Quality
- `lindvb --self-check` checks the proxy lookup, listens to the decoded sound (a silent stream fails) and plays an HTTPS HLS stream through the timeshift buffer as an IPTV channel plays (#182, #183)
