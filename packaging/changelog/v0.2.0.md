## What's new in 0.2.0

### DVB-I (internet TV with a DVB channel list)
- Channel list from the DVB-I service list registry (#24)
- Programme guide from the DVB-I content guide, ahead of XMLTV, with cropped channel logos (#27)
- Failover between service instances, direct satellite tuning, CI+ and encrypted instances, pop-up channels, TTML subtitles and a softcam setup guide (#28)

### Radio and visualisations
- TV and radio are separate lists, and the last channel of each is remembered (#12)
- Radio visualisations, now drawn on the GPU, including Goom HD with the original GOOM logic and a hang watchdog (#12, #16, #21)
- New visualisations: light ribbons and pulsar (#23)
- Visualisation picker button with a sine wave icon in the control bar (#17, #22)
- Station logo, album cover, song title and clock over the visualisation (#18)

### Recording
- Full recording timer dialog with TS, MPG, MP4 and MKV formats and extra streams (#13)
- Recording format and audio track options in Preferences, timers and instant recordings (#20)
- Recordings tab next to TV and Radio in the channel list (#19)
- HLS/DASH internet streams record with picture and sound in every format (#25)

### Programme guide (EPG)
- Provider EPG for MediaHighway and Freesat, and XMLTV channel matching (#11)
- EPG settings have their own page in Preferences (#15)
- The guide preselects and scrolls to the playing channel in every view (#26)

### Player and interface
- Redesigned player with a minimal dark look and one blue accent colour throughout (#9, #10)
- Teletext button next to the guide button in the header bar (#22)
- Extended info bar and fixed A/V sync on HLS streams (#14)

### Conditional access and plugins
- Plugin system and conditional access for your own channels (#7)
- CAM menu, teletext subtitles and plugin switches (#8)

### Packaging
- Install notes in the release and README, including how to install the unsigned snap (#6)
