## What's new in 0.19.0

### Channel list
- The filter, group and tag dropdowns become a filter in four linked steps, each offering only what suits the steps before it: source (satellite, cable, terrestrial, internet streams, DVB-I, each Tvheadend server), area, filter (free to air / encrypted, picture quality, Tvheadend tags) and sort order. A button resets the steps and the search; the choices are kept for TV and radio separately, and the settings of older versions are taken over (#153, #150)
- Step 2 shows the satellites of an orbital position below it and the channels of a terrestrial region ("K 23 · 490 MHz"); ticking a position stands for all its satellites (#154)
- ATSC and ISDB-T channels numbered like "7.1" sit under their station ("7 · WABC") and are labelled on their 6 MHz raster ("RF 14 · 473 MHz"); DTMB channels by frequency. ISDB-T services for phones (1seg) are a quality of their own (#166)
- Picture quality (UHD, HD, SD) no longer goes by the channel name alone: a channel remembers the picture size seen while watching it, before that its DVB service type counts (from the channel search, enigma2 lamedb, Tvheadend and the SDT while the channel plays) (#156, #166)
- Favourites get a tab of their own: the channel list has TV, Radio, Favourites and Recordings tabs, shown as symbols; Ctrl+D opens the favourites. The tab shows one favourites list in its own order, TV and radio together, with a list choice and a button that opens it in the channel editor (#152)
- The "Picture quality" sort is now called "Video resolution" (#142)

### Tvheadend
- The channel tags have a choice of their own, and the values of the other sorts come from the channels shown, not from the whole channel list (#138, #136)
- The networks of the channels (satellite positions, cable or antenna networks) are read over HTSP (port 9982, no admin rights needed) and offered in step 2 of the list filter (#155)
- Channel lists are fetched again at the start after a day, and after an update of LinDVB, so channels stored by an older version get their tags (#152, #111)
- The now/next in the on-screen display no longer sticks to the first channel watched when the server gives every channel the same service id (#140, #126)

### Reception
- DiSEqC 1.1: up to 16 satellites per tuner, with a 1.1 switch or uncommitted switches alone or in front of 1.0 switches (Preferences › Tuners › DiSEqC switch). The channel search shows how many satellites a tuner's reception path tells apart, and Unicable II / JESS positions above 4 can be chosen now (#151)

### Playback
- Timeshift: choose off, in memory (RAM, in /dev/shm, at most half of the RAM) or on disk (as before). The size field shows how long the buffer lasts, and buffers left behind by a crash are removed at start (#143)
- Timeshift for internet radio: pause, jump back and keep the buffer as a recording, with the song titles shown at the place they ran (#163)
- Audio passthrough: Dolby Digital (AC-3), Dolby Digital Plus (E-AC-3) and DTS go undecoded over HDMI or S/PDIF to an AV receiver, through PulseAudio / PipeWire or straight to an ALSA device (Preferences › Playback › Audio passthrough) (#164)
- "Keep Timeshift as Recording" works again (#143)

### On-screen display and programme guide
- The on-screen display gets a technical row: where the channel comes from (DVB-S/S2, DVB-C, DVB-T/T2, ATSC, ISDB-T, DTMB, SAT>IP, Tvheadend, DVB-I, IPTV), resolution (SD 576i, HD 1080i, UHD 2160p), 16:9 or 4:3, HDR, the sound on offer (mono, stereo, 5.1, 7.1, codec, languages, audio description), subtitles, a symbol for programmes in black and white and the minutes left (#142, #127)
- The programme details show the whole description, which scrolls on its own below the title; the divider position is remembered (#144)

### HbbTV
- While an application runs it gets the keys ahead of a focused button or the channel list, so Enter and the arrows reach the teletext and the media libraries instead of pressing the TXT or HbbTV button again (#158)
- The teletext of ARD's launcher stays open instead of closing right after it starts (#148)
- Double-click toggles full screen again over an application, and no text cursor shows over the picture (#137)

### Internet and DVB-I
- DVB-I services of ARD (ARD alpha, BR Fernsehen, tagesschau24, Radio Bremen) that failed with "Forbidden (403)" play: stream requests identify as an HbbTV terminal and follow the gateway's redirects (#149)
- A refused stream names the server that refused it ("… refused the stream (HTTP 403)") instead of "Internal data stream error" (#160)
- Preferences › Internet: the IPTV section comes first, DVB-I last (#159)

### Bug reports and feedback
- Debug mode (Preferences › Debug): writes `~/.cache/lindvb/debug.log` for bug reports, with versions, tuning, playback, recordings, HbbTV and errors, and without passwords, PINs or your user name; buttons to open the folder, save a copy and delete the log. It takes in the HbbTV diagnostic log (#147, #145, #139)
- The About dialog has a Feedback section with the mail address and the GitHub issues and discussions (#162)

### Languages
- Armenian and Thai translations; LinDVB now speaks 34 languages (#146, #161)
