## What's new in 0.13.0

### Channel search
- The current transponder lists of dtv-scan-tables are built in (commit 97129f5, June 2025), so the search works on every distribution and in the AppImage, Snap and Flatpak without extra packages (#89)
- The dialog shows only what the tuner receives. Satellite: orbital positions with a DiSEqC port each (one position covers every satellite there, e.g. 19.2°E for Astra 1KR, 1L, 1M, 1N) and the LNB. Cable: country and provider. Terrestrial, ATSC and ISDB-T: country and region. No LNB or DiSEqC for cable and terrestrial tuners (#89)
- Blind scan over all frequencies of the region's channel raster, for countries without a list (#89)
- Several satellite positions behind a DiSEqC switch in one search, also on single-cable (Unicable/JESS) tuners (#89)
- DVBViewer channel lists keep their DiSEqC ports (#81)

### Channels and tuners
- Favourites: any number of named lists, each sorted freely (#88)
- Picons: download only the logos of your channels, and find logos named after the channel, like "Bayern 3.png" (#87)
- Smooth scrolling in very large channel lists (#84)
- Tuner settings: a switch for the shared antenna cable, and Unicable details only when a standard is chosen (#80, #85)
- Sorting by category (#81)

### Interface
- Recording timers: cleaner, better structured layout (#82)
- DVB-I settings: the privacy notice opens in its own window, no overlapping text (#83)
- Preferences use the full width in every language and scroll from the top (#86)

### Fixes
- CA certificates in the AppImage (#81)
