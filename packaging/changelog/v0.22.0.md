## What's new in 0.22.0

### Subtitles and sound of internet channels
- HLS subtitle and audio renditions: extra languages and "Klare Sprache" (ARD-alpha) are offered and play, with timeshift too. Subtitles are off by default, and "Off" really switches them off (#196)
- Closed captions (CEA-708) are offered only when the video carries them, and they are now actually drawn, more smoothly (#188, #196)
- Text subtitles (WebVTT, TTML, SRT) are drawn in the Snap, the .deb and the AppImage: they now bring the text overlay (#198)
- The sound menu names AAC, MP2 and MP3 instead of "MPEG" and marks audio description by its language, its name or the playlist's description (#198)
- Tracks no longer show twice in the menus, and PBS channels no longer loop between subtitles on and off (#198)

### Internet TV
- PBS streams no longer stall in the timeshift buffer, and HLS playlists that the server sends gzip-compressed play (#194, #195)
- A stream that stops delivering is opened again after 20 seconds, instead of only at the start (#198)
- DVB-I channels with timeshift get every sound of a DASH stream and their HbbTV from the service list (ONE HD) (#198)
- Channel analysis measures internet channels too, from the timeshift buffer (#190)

### Programme guide
- Preferences > EPG keeps a list of internet guides (XMLTV) from web addresses and files: add, update, rename and remove them (#192)
- IPTV playlists imported from the main menu are remembered under Preferences > Internet > Your lists (#192)
- Busy cable networks that send the whole schedule on one PID show the guide of the tuned transponder again (#189)

### Interface
- The channel list scrolls to the tuned channel, is never empty at the start and its rows no longer change height (#193)
- Switching between TV, radio, favourites and recordings clears the search (#198)
- Recordings and screenshots go into the desktop's own Videos and Pictures folders. The recording, screenshot and timeshift folders have a button that opens the folder chooser (#198)

### Snap
- Recordings and screenshots go into your real ~/Videos and ~/Pictures. Before, they went into ~/snap/lindvb/<revision>, which snapd deletes with old revisions. Recordings already made there still show in the recordings list. Moving them to ~/Videos/LinDVB keeps them for good (#198)

### AppImage
- The AppImage brings its own glibc, so it runs on Ubuntu 22.04 too (#191)

### Quality
- A regression net of tests for streams, timeshift, subtitles and extra audio, and a check that measures the player's CPU, memory, threads and open files on every pull request (#197)
