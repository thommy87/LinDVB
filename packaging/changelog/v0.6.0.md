## What's new in 0.6.0

### HbbTV 2.0.4 (red button)
- Interactive services of the channels start from the broadcast signalling (AIT), with the red button, the colour keys and the key sets apps ask for (#41)
- Apps run in a WebKitGTK browser over the video and get the OIPF objects of HbbTV 2.0.4: the broadcast video in a window of its own, channel list and channel change, audio and subtitle tracks, configuration and capabilities (#41)
- Videos of the media libraries play inside LinDVB, MPEG-DASH included, with a choice of audio language, audio description and EBU-TT-D subtitles (#42)
- Apps and files can come from the broadcast itself (DSM-CC object carousel, `dvb://` URLs), and apps can react to stream events, for example to quiz questions during a show (#43)
- The TXT key (Ctrl+Shift+T) opens the channel's digital teletext where there is one (#43)
- Youth protection: an age limit and a PIN in the settings. Apps and videos rated above the limit only start after the PIN is entered, and apps cannot switch the protection off (#43)
- DVB-I internet channels get HbbTV too: the service list links the applications (XML AIT or app page), and services that are an application of their own appear in the channel list (#44)
- Companion screens: apps can talk to apps on a phone or tablet (app to app, DIAL) and keep their videos in sync with the TV (CSS-CII, CSS-WC, CSS-TS). These network services are off until you switch them on in the settings (#43)
- Encrypted videos (Widevine, PlayReady, Marlin) cannot play because LinDVB has no DRM. A notice now says so, instead of the app hanging (#43)
- The AppImage now includes WebKitGTK, so HbbTV works without extra packages (#43)
- On desktops where WebKit's sandbox cannot start (Ubuntu 24.04 with its default user namespace restriction), HbbTV switches off and says why (#42)
