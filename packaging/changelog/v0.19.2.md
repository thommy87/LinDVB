## What's new in 0.19.2

### Snap
- H.264 and the other libav decoders work in the snap: the GStreamer libav plugin did not load, because two libraries it needs (BLAS and LAPACK) were not on the snap's library path, and LinDVB reported "avdec_h264" as missing (#169)
- PulseAudio output in the snap no longer depends on a library the 0.19.1 clean-up step had removed (#169)

### Quality
- New `lindvb --self-check`: starts GTK, looks for the GStreamer elements live TV needs and plays a short H.264/MPEG audio test stream. Every package (AppImage, Flatpak, .deb, AUR, Snap) is now installed and checked this way before it is released
- The snap goes to the `candidate` channel after Canonical's build passed the check on amd64 and arm64; `stable` follows only after it was tried by hand
