## What's new in 0.19.2

### Snap
- H.264 and the other libav decoders work in the snap: the GStreamer libav plugin did not load, because two libraries it needs (BLAS and LAPACK) were not on the snap's library path, and LinDVB reported "avdec_h264" as missing (#169)
- PulseAudio output in the snap no longer depends on a library the 0.19.1 clean-up step had removed (#169)
