## What's new in 0.23.0

### Subtitles
- LinDVB loads and draws the WebVTT subtitles of HLS channels itself, so picture and sound no longer wait for them (#233, #236, #239)
- Closed captions (CEA-608 and CEA-708) are drawn by LinDVB, as on a TV and in time with their frame. Channels with CEA-608 captions only offer them too (#235, #237, #238)
- HLS channels keep their timeshift with subtitles switched on: the subtitles go into the timeshift buffer. This is on by default (Preferences > Timeshift) (#240, #241, #243)
- Recordings keep the subtitles of HLS channels: an MKV recording gets a text track, a TS recording a .srt file beside it, in time with the picture (#242)
- DASH subtitles picked during playback come at once (#223, #224)

### Sound and programme features
- New symbols in the on-screen display: original language (two rings) and clear speech (the subtitle ear with a level meter, for sound tracks such as "Klare Sprache") (#244)
- The programme details of the guide show what a programme offers, with the same symbols: black and white, HD, 4:3, mono, stereo, 5.1, original language, clear speech, audio description, subtitles and sign language, as the broadcast guide, XMLTV or DVB-I signal them (#244)
- A preferred audio language and a choice for audio description during playback (#204)
- 3sat: every sound of the playlist once, by name, with audio description (#213)

### Timeshift
- Timeshift is a switch of its own in Preferences > Timeshift (on by default), and the buffer (memory or disk) a choice beside it. Switching timeshift on again switches the subtitles in the buffer on again (#243)
- The buffer starts again when the muxer fails on its first output (#215)

### DVB-I
- The country list of DVB-I, with a question before the country changes, and an HbbTV country of its own. Further lists are added beside the country's list (#203)
- A refused stream switches to another instance of the channel instead of going direct, and subtitles can come from the broadcast instance (#210)

### Fixes
- Every channel change left files open, which crashed LinDVB after many changes (#212, #219)
- New installations start in the desktop's light or dark look (#213)
- 3sat HD starts reliably, and NDR's sound language no longer trips the muxer (#214)

### Quality
- New parts first ran in the shadow beside the old ones and were compared before they took over: the track catalogue, one state model for all sources, and the subtitle renderers for teletext, DVB subtitles, closed captions and text cues (#208, #209, #211, #221, #222, #225, #227 to #231)
- Channel analysis shows what the closed captions in the video carry (#228)
- The debug mode logs more for picture and graphics problems: open files, live pipelines, graphics buffers, and a VA-API test (--va-test) (#215 to #220, #232)
